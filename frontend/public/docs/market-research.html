GEO AI Visibility Dashboard

Market research and possible evolution — August 2026
What the research says
The AI visibility market is moving beyond a simple visibility score. Current products are adding large-scale prompt intelligence, competitor and citation analysis, prompt-gap research and prioritized actions. For our dashboard, the opportunity is to understand the context behind visibility — especially for pharma — rather than simply adding another recommendation layer.

2. A few important lessons from the research

Visibility is not the same as influenceSemrush's own 2026 experience found its content could be cited hundreds of times while competitors were still recommended. Citation reach does not necessarily mean the brand is winning the answer.
Prompt coverage can be wrongProfound's Prompt Research Reports exist partly because traditional prompt tracking can reflect what teams think users ask rather than what users actually ask.
Citation data has limitsBing explicitly says citation activity is aggregated/sampled and does not establish causality. Changes can come from user demand, content changes, model updates and partner refresh cycles.
Different data sources answer different questionsProfound uses real-user prompt data; Ahrefs uses a large search-backed prompt index; Bing reports first-party citation activity. These signals should complement each other, not be treated as equivalent datasets.

4. Why the pharma context matters

The business discussion already raised questions around competitor definition, multiple countries, focus/no-focus decisions and real patient/HCP questions. These are not just additional filters; they provide the context needed to interpret AI visibility.

Molecule × Indication × Lifecycle stage × Patient / HCP context × Country ↓ Relevant competitive set ↓ Real / observed AI questions ↓ Brand + competitor visibility ↓ Citations + recommendation position ↓ Opportunity

Example: the relevant competitor for a molecule may differ by indication, treatment setting, lifecycle stage or country. A country with lower visibility may not automatically be a problem if AI demand or strategic relevance is also low.

5. What the dashboard could evolve toward

MARKET CONTEXT ↓ AI QUESTIONS + INTENT ↓ VISIBILITY + COMPETITORS + CITATIONS ↓ WHY / WHAT IS DRIVING IT? ↓ OPPORTUNITY ↓ FOCUS / MONITOR / LOWER PRIORITY ↓ MEASURE CHANGE

The aim would be to make the existing analytics more useful, not simply make the dashboard larger.
